Zhexu Alex Jin

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate at the statistics department in University of Illinois, Urbana Champaign. I am advised by Dr. Joshua Agterberg.

I study the computational and statistical limits of machine learning and nonconvex optimization. I use tools from high-dimensional statistics, random matrix theory, riemannian optimization and complexity theory to understand when and why learning algorithms succeed and to develop more reliable methods.

I recieved my data science Bachelor degree at Duke Kunshan University and Duke University. In my undergraduate years, I worked with Dr. Dongmian Zou and Dr. Ivan Mura on geometric machine learning and stochastic modeling.
